Thinking

Simple on purpose.

The goal is not to fill every format with information. It is to make the offer easy to understand, keep the business recognisable and let the same campaign adapt naturally across different placements.

01 / Brief

Drive morning visits.

Create a straightforward promotional campaign for the café's quieter early-morning period.

02 / Idea

One message.

Keep the communication centred around one memorable line and one easy-to-understand offer.

03 / System

Built to resize.

Use a simple visual structure that can move from square ads to horizontal banners without losing clarity.
