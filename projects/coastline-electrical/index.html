Thinking

Clear beats clever.

For an urgent local service, the campaign should not make the customer work to understand what is being offered. The creative stays simple, high-contrast and action-focused while still giving the business a recognisable visual identity.

01 / Brief

Generate local enquiries.

Promote same-day electrical callouts and turn ad views into booking requests.

02 / Message

Lead with the problem.

Use language that mirrors the customer's immediate need instead of generic brand messaging.

03 / System

Make the CTA obvious.

Keep every format centred on one next step: request or book an electrician.
